01The invitation

A classroom without walls.

Come to Kenya for the places. Stay for the perspective.

The African Summer Immersion brings students into a different kind of learning environment — one shaped by culture, conversation, creativity, movement and everyday life in Kenya.

You’ll explore Nairobi, spend time in academic spaces, meet people, encounter Kenya’s history and institutions, and experience landscapes and wildlife that turn a summer abroad into something you can actually feel.

01 / Nairobi

Meet Nairobi
at street level.

Start with the city as it moves. A matatu ride puts you close to Nairobi’s rhythm, colour, pace and everyday life.

02 / Culture

Start
with the story.

At the National Museum of Kenya, slow down long enough to understand the place you’re entering through history, heritage, art and natural story.

03 / Perspective

Where Kenya
meets the world.

See Nairobi through a wider lens, from global institutions to the civic spaces where ideas, policy and international conversations meet.
