01How the itinerary works

Not a timetable.
A journey through Kenya.

The itinerary is designed as a sequence of experiences rather than a stack of activities. Each stop adds something different: movement, context, exchange, craft, wildlife, landscape and time to reflect.

The exact day by day schedule can be confirmed separately. This page shows the shape of the immersion and the experiences that make it distinctive.

Meet Nairobi

Culture · ContextDiscover

Start with

Slow down at the National Museum of Kenya and begin to understand the place through history, heritage, art and natural story. The experience moves from seeing Kenya to beginning to understand it.

HistoryHeritagePerspective

part of the classroom.

Step into academic environments at Strathmore University Kenya and USIU Africa. Exchange perspectives, experience another university setting and let the place itself become part of what you are learning.
